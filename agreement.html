The work

What you are getting into.

Same system Kraken publishes on the site: diamond grinding, crack repair, moisture testing, an epoxy base, decorative flake to full coverage, and a polyaspartic topcoat. A typical garage is installed in as little as one day. Your proposal holds the schedule for your slab.

1. The concrete, as it is

Cracks get sealed. Moisture gets tested. The coating goes on concrete that has been opened up, not on a dirty slab with a quick wash.

2. Diamond grinding

The floor is ground mechanically so the coating can bond. Kraken does not acid-etch this system.

3. Edges and detail

Open floor is not enough. The edges, where the slab meets the wall, get ground so the coating is not thin where tires turn.

4. Flake and the pour

Decorative flake goes into the base coat to full coverage. A clear polyaspartic topcoat locks it in. It is UV-stable, built for hot tires, salt, and a Montana winter. Watch the pour before you sign.

    Warranty

    What the guarantee depends on.

    Kraken publishes a lifetime adhesion warranty on this coating. It stays in force when the floor is prepared the way this page describes, and when you contact Kraken inside the notice window if something is wrong.

    The adhesion warranty is void if:

    • Something is wrong with the coating and you do not contact Kraken within the notice window written on this job.
    • The work area was not cleared, so the concrete could not be prepared the way this agreement requires.
    • Belongings were left in the work area.
    • You were not ready at the scheduled start, and the crew could not do that preparation.
